# Stage 5 — Cart

Date: 2026-09-19

## What was built

- **Persistent multi-vendor cart** at `/cart`, grouped by vendor, with per-vendor subtotals, quantity stepper,
  remove, personalisation shown per line, live price/stock re-evaluation, and clear states for
  *unavailable* (delisted / vendor suspended / option removed), *out of stock*, and *limited* (qty > stock).
- **Guest carts** via an httpOnly `gp_cart` cookie (256-bit random token, 30 days). Guests have no RLS path by design, so
  guest carts are read/written with the service-role client, but *only* ever resolved from that cookie's token.
  Signed-in carts use the shopper's own session client, so RLS is the enforcement there.
- **Guest → account merge**: `merge_guest_cart()` (SQL, SECURITY DEFINER, service_role-only) runs atomically under a row
  lock. Identical lines (same product + variants + personalisation) sum; others move across; the guest cart is deleted.
  Triggered from sign-in, sign-up-with-session and the OAuth callback, plus a safety net (`GuestCartMerger` on the cart
  page, and merge-before-write in cart mutations) for other ways to end up signed in with a guest cookie.
- **PDP wired up**: Add to Cart / Buy Now (Buy Now adds then opens `/cart` until Stage 6 checkout exists), personalisation
  fields are now real (they were uncontrolled inputs), navbar badge shows the unit count.
- `lib/commerce/pricing.ts` (pure, tested): `computeUnitPrice`, `effectiveStock`, `maxPurchasable`, `lineTotal`. Checkout in
  Stage 6 must reuse these so a line can't cost one thing in the cart and another at checkout.

## Decisions

- **`cart_items.variant_ids uuid[]`** added. A product may have several variant groups ("Wrapping Style" + "Size") and the PDP
  lets you pick one per group; a single `variant_id` column couldn't hold that. `variant_id` is kept (FK, `order_items`
  compatibility) and mirrored from `variant_ids[1]` by the trigger. **Stage 6 will need the same array on `order_items`.**
- **The DB is the authority on what a cart line references.** `cart_items_enforce_catalog` (SECURITY INVOKER, per the Stage 2
  rule) rejects non-active products, copies `vendor_id` from the product, and validates variants (belong to product, active,
  one per group). Fires on insert / `update of product_id, variant_ids` only, so a shopper can still lower the quantity of or
  remove a delisted line. `unit_price_snapshot` is display-only ("price updated" notice); nothing trusts it.
- Limits: 99 per line (DB CHECK), 20 per line (app, `MAX_LINE_QUANTITY`), 50 lines per cart, personalisation ≤ 2000 bytes, ≤ 5 variants.
- Cart writes rate-limited (60/min per IP, `cartRateLimit`) since guests can create cart rows.
- Effective stock = min(product stock, each chosen variant's stock).

## Bug found and fixed on the way (not part of the cart itself)

`signInAction` / the OAuth callback validated the post-login redirect with `startsWith("/")`, which accepts `//evil.com`
(protocol-relative) — an open redirect. Replaced by `lib/auth/redirect.ts` `safeRedirectPath()` (tested).

## Verification

- As a real signed-in customer through the anon key (RLS): can create own cart; `vendor_id` forced from product; inactive
  product / foreign-product variant / two options of one group / qty > 99 / oversized personalisation all rejected; cannot write
  to or read another user's cart; anon sees no carts; anon and customers cannot call `merge_guest_cart`.
- Merge semantics: identical lines sum (1+2=3), personalised line stays separate, guest cart deleted, re-run is a no-op,
  works when the account has no cart yet.
- Browser: guest add (variant + personalisation, price ₹2,999+₹150 = ₹3,149 correct), stepper, subtotal + badge; sign-in
  merged the guest cart (guest cart row gone, items on the account cart); delisted product → "no longer available"; stock cut
  to 2 with qty 4 → "Only 2 available" until lowered; adding past stock refused server-side; identical add increments,
  different recipient makes a new line; remove → empty state; 375px width has no horizontal overflow.
- `lint`, `tsc`, `npm test` (26 tests), `npm run build` clean.
